# Refero reference: BRIMMUP

Checker: https://styles.refero.design/?ref=ProductDisrupt (homepage listing of 20 styles, reviewed 9 Oct 2026).

## Research brief

- Category: RETAIL (caps). Primary action: WhatsApp enquiry.
- Starting direction: dark product editorial (see `brand-analysis.md`).
- Questions:
  1. Which dark palette keeps warm product photos looking rich without a pure-black ground?
  2. How to make one action colour stand out on a dark page?
  3. What type treatment carries a bold uppercase brand without poster clutter?

## Shortlist

From the listing, dark styles considered: Factory, Raycast, ORYZO AI, Apple iPhone 18 Pro, AI for Business. Factory and Raycast are terminal/software moods with cool neon accents (rejected: wrong temperature for walnut-and-gold). AI for Business: no colour data on the card (not inspected).

### Reference 1: ORYZO AI

- **URL:** https://styles.refero.design/style/1f204e95-454a-437e-845b-c1b169d35607
- **Research question:** 1 and 3.
- **Observed patterns:** warm-dark canvas `#100904`; cream text `#ffedd7`, never pure white; uppercase weight-500 labels and headings; 1px dashed dividers `#40372e`; depth from surface steps, no shadows; one filled pill button per section; products isolated on the dark ground.
- **Useful principle:** a warm void plus cream type makes warm product photos the only colour on the page.
- **Client adaptation:** adopt canvas, cream text, dashed dividers, no shadows, uppercase headings, one filled button per section.
- **What not to copy:** its low-contrast bark-brown CTA (conversion needs a bright action); 100vh sections (too long on mobile); the ORYZO wordmark and 3D renders.
- **Mobile assessment:** not verified (style page only).
- **Decision:** Adapt.

### Reference 2: Apple iPhone 18 Pro

- **URL:** https://styles.refero.design/style/40be36d7-7fe6-4451-9f2d-7ceccfd43be8
- **Research question:** 2.
- **Observed patterns:** one saturated colour reserved for purchase controls only; muted grey for metadata; media without shadows.
- **Useful principle:** the action colour appears nowhere except on actions, so it reads as "tap here".
- **Client adaptation:** Brimmup gold is used only on the WhatsApp button and focus ring.
- **What not to copy:** Apple blue, SF Pro, 28px media radii, product names and layout.
- **Mobile assessment:** not verified.
- **Decision:** Adapt.

## Conflicts resolved

ORYZO keeps its CTA low-key; Apple makes it the brightest element. Chose Apple's approach: the WhatsApp enquiry is the storefront's only goal (priority 1, project scope).

## Unresolved

- Mobile behaviour of both references not verified; Refero Styles shows style pages, not live sites.
