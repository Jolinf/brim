## Current presentation

- Products are clear in the posters, but every poster carries text, icons and the phone number, so few images work as product thumbnails. Only three images are clean product shots.
- Contact details are consistent (07026987717, @brimmup) across all but one poster.
- The previous site misnamed the brand, showed three missing images, and repeated unsourced claims.

## Preserve, improve, introduce

- **Preserve:** BRIMMUP uppercase name and crown; dark warm grounds; gold accent; condensed uppercase headlines; "Wear the mindset".
- **Improve:** one clean image per collection instead of text-heavy posters; one consistent type hierarchy; a single, clear WhatsApp action.
- **Introduce (recommendation):** cream text on walnut black instead of pure white on black; dashed hairline dividers; colour names listed per collection.

## Visual direction

Primary: **dark product editorial**. Warm walnut-black page, cream uppercase type, gold reserved for the WhatsApp action, product photos on the dark ground without frames or shadows. Supports the posters' mood and keeps the page fast.
